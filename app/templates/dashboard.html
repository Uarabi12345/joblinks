Frequently Asked Questions

How does the payment work?

You pay a small "Unlock Fee" to access the client's contact link for a specific job. Once unlocked, the job is yours, and you keep 100% of the "Payout" amount the client gives you.

Can two people claim the same job?

No. Once a job is unlocked, it locks for everyone else. It's first come, first served.

How do I get paid?

Payment is arranged directly between you and the client after you claim the job. Most clients pay via M-Pesa within 24 hours.
